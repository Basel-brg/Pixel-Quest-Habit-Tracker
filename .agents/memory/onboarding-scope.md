---
name: Introducing account onboarding
description: Why existing players were exempted when first-run onboarding was introduced.
---

Treat first-run setup as a new-account experience. Preserve established players' access and progress rather than forcing them to create another starter habit and reward.

**Why:** The requested flow is for newly registered users. Introducing it to an existing product must not reset established play or make the starter XP repeatable.

**How to apply:** Preserve the one-time exemption for accounts that predate onboarding. Never clear completion flags merely to preview or retest the wizard; use a verified dedicated fresh test account.
